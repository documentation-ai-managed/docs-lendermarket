---
title: Repayments and payment allocation
description: >-
  Every payment you report with createLoanPayment is allocated by the platform.
  You send the total amount received. The platform decides how much of it is
  delayed interest, interest and principal, records the result on the loan and
  on every investment in the loan, and returns the split to you. This page
  explains the allocation rules, how interest and delayed interest are
  calculated, and what happens when a payment is on time, late, early, partial
  or larger than the amount due.
---
## What you send and what you get back

`POST createLoanPayment` takes the loan and the total amount received.

| Field           | Meaning                                              |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| `loanUuid`      | The loan the payment is for                          |
| `paymentAmount` | The total amount received. The platform allocates it |
| `currency`      | Must equal the loan currency                         |

The amount must be greater than zero and must not have more decimals than the currency allows (two for EUR). The loan must be `ACTIVE`.

The response shows how the platform allocated the payment.

```json
{
  "data": {
    "uuid": "7a0f3c9e-4b2d-4c1e-9f7a-2d8e5b6c1a10",
    "loanUuid": "c2f1e8d4-7b3a-4e5f-8c9d-1a2b3c4d5e6f",
    "principalAmount": "300.00",
    "interestAmount": "8.10",
    "delayedInterestAmount": "0.00",
    "paymentDate": "2027-02-14",
    "currency": "EUR"
  }
}
```

`paymentDate` is the date on which the platform received the API call (UTC). Payments cannot be backdated. If a borrower paid you on an earlier date, the platform still applies the payment, calculates interest and counts days of delay as of the date you report it.

## Allocation order

The platform walks through the unpaid installments of the loan from the oldest due date to the newest. Within each installment it settles the claims in a fixed order.

| Step | Claim            | Rule                                                           |
| ---- | ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Delayed interest | Interest accrued on principal that was paid after its due date |
| 2    | Interest         | The scheduled interest of the installment                      |
| 3    | Principal        | The scheduled principal of the installment                     |

The amount moves to the next step only when the current claim is fully settled, and to the next installment only when all three claims of the current installment are settled. A payment therefore never reduces principal while interest or delayed interest of the same or an earlier installment is still outstanding.

Principal is settled pro rata across the investments in the installment and your own retained share of the loan (`lenderSkinPercentage`). The response reports loan-level amounts. The per-investment split is handled by the platform and is not returned.

A payment larger than the installments due on the payment date continues into the following installments. Interest of a future installment is only charged up to the payment date, and the rest of the amount reduces the principal of that installment early (see the early repayment examples below). Any amount left after all outstanding claims of the loan are settled is recorded as an overpayment. It is not allocated to future installments and is not returned automatically. Contact your Lendermarket account manager to reconcile an overpayment.

## How interest is calculated

Interest is simple interest on the outstanding principal, calculated per installment period.

| Parameter  | Value                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rate       | The loan `interestRate`, converted to a daily rate as `interestRate / 100 / 360`                                                                                                |
| Year basis | 360 days                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Day count  | Calendar days between the previous due date and the installment due date. For the first installment, from the date the loan was created on the platform                         |
| Rounding   | Truncated (not rounded) to the currency precision. Truncation is applied to the cumulative interest of the loan so that cents lost on one installment are carried into the next |

The platform calculates the interest of every installment from the `schedule` you send in `createLoan` and rejects a schedule whose interest amounts do not match. Use the same method on your side.

Example: a loan of EUR 900.00 at 10.80 % with three installments of EUR 300.00, 30 days apart.

| Installment | Outstanding principal | Days | Interest                    |
| ----------- | --------------------- | ---- | --------------------------- |
| 1           | 900.00                | 30   | 900.00 × 0.0003 × 30 = 8.10 |
| 2           | 600.00                | 30   | 600.00 × 0.0003 × 30 = 5.40 |
| 3           | 300.00                | 30   | 300.00 × 0.0003 × 30 = 2.70 |

The daily rate in this example is 10.80 / 100 / 360 = 0.0003.

## How delayed interest is calculated

Delayed interest starts on the day after an installment's due date and runs until the overdue principal of that installment is paid. It uses the same daily rate as the loan.

| Parameter | Value                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Base      | The overdue principal of the installment that is held by investors. Your own retained share and any part of the installment that was not funded by investors do not accrue delayed interest |
| Rate      | The loan daily rate (`interestRate / 100 / 360`)                                                                                                                                            |
| Day count | Calendar days from the due date to the payment date, or to today for an installment that is still unpaid                                                                                    |
| Rounding  | Truncated to the currency precision per investment, then summed for the loan                                                                                                                |

Delayed interest is recalculated once a day for every overdue loan and again at the moment you report a payment, so the amount you see in `getLoan` and the amount allocated by `createLoanPayment` are both current as of the payment date.

If a payment covers only part of the overdue principal, the remaining part keeps accruing delayed interest until it is paid.

## Where to see the current amounts

`GET getLoan` returns the schedule with the current state of every installment.

| Field                                                                                         | Meaning                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `totalPrincipalAmount`, `totalInterestAmount`, `totalDelayedInterestAmount`                   | Amounts due for the installment                                              |
| `paidPrincipalAmount`, `paidInterestAmount`, `paidDelayedInterestAmount`                      | Amounts settled so far                                                       |
| `outstandingPrincipalAmount`, `outstandingInterestAmount`, `outstandingDelayedInterestAmount` | Amounts still open                                                           |
| `accruedInterestAmount`                                                                       | Interest earned on the installment up to today, whether or not it is due yet |

Before sending a payment, read `getLoan` and add up the outstanding amounts of every installment with a due date up to and including the payment date. That is the amount that settles the loan to date.

## Use cases

All examples use the EUR 900.00 loan at 10.80 % from above, created on the platform on 2027-01-15, with installments due on 2027-02-14, 2027-03-16 and 2027-04-15. Investors hold 90 % of the loan (EUR 810.00) and you retain 10 %. Delayed interest amounts assume a single investment of EUR 810.00. With several investments the per-investment truncation can make the loan total a few cents lower.

### On-time payment

Installment 1 is due on 2027-02-14. You report EUR 308.10 on that day.

```json
{
  "loanUuid": "c2f1e8d4-7b3a-4e5f-8c9d-1a2b3c4d5e6f",
  "paymentAmount": "308.10",
  "currency": "EUR"
}
```

| Step | Claim            | Outstanding | Allocated | Remaining payment |
| ---- | ---------------- | ----------- | --------- | ----------------- |
| 1    | Delayed interest | 0.00        | 0.00      | 308.10            |
| 2    | Interest         | 8.10        | 8.10      | 300.00            |
| 3    | Principal        | 300.00      | 300.00    | 0.00              |

Response: `principalAmount` 300.00, `interestAmount` 8.10, `delayedInterestAmount` 0.00. Installment 1 is closed. Installments 2 and 3 are unchanged.

### Late payment

Installment 1 is not paid on 2027-02-14. You report EUR 308.10 on 2027-02-26, 12 days late.

Before the payment is allocated the platform calculates delayed interest on the overdue investor-held principal: 270.00 × 0.0003 × 12 days = 0.972, truncated to EUR 0.97.

| Step | Claim            | Outstanding | Allocated | Remaining payment |
| ---- | ---------------- | ----------- | --------- | ----------------- |
| 1    | Delayed interest | 0.97        | 0.97      | 307.13            |
| 2    | Interest         | 8.10        | 8.10      | 299.03            |
| 3    | Principal        | 300.00      | 299.03    | 0.00              |

Response: `principalAmount` 299.03, `interestAmount` 8.10, `delayedInterestAmount` 0.97. Installment 1 stays open with EUR 0.97 principal outstanding, which continues to accrue delayed interest until it is paid.

To close the installment in one payment, read `getLoan` on the payment date and send the sum of the outstanding amounts, here EUR 309.07.

An installment that stays unpaid longer than the `buybackTermInDays` of your loan publishing rule triggers an automatic loan buyback. See the buyback page for the amounts involved.

### Several installments overdue

Installments 1 and 2 are both unpaid on 2027-03-26. You report EUR 500.00. The payment is applied to installment 1 first (delayed interest, interest, principal), then to installment 2 in the same order. If the amount runs out during installment 2, the unpaid remainder of installment 2 stays outstanding and keeps accruing delayed interest. Installment 3 is not touched until installments 1 and 2 are fully settled.

### Early full repayment

You report the full outstanding balance on 2027-01-30, 15 days after the loan was created and before any installment is due.

The platform recalculates the interest of the loan up to the payment date, because the borrower only owes interest for the days the principal was outstanding: 900.00 × 0.0003 × 15 = 4.05. The original scheduled interest of 8.10, 5.40 and 2.70 is revoked. The amount that settles the loan is EUR 904.05.

| Step | Claim                   | Outstanding | Allocated | Remaining payment |
| ---- | ----------------------- | ----------- | --------- | ----------------- |
| 1    | Delayed interest        | 0.00        | 0.00      | 904.05            |
| 2    | Interest (recalculated) | 4.05        | 4.05      | 900.00            |
| 3    | Principal               | 900.00      | 900.00    | 0.00              |

Response: `principalAmount` 900.00, `interestAmount` 4.05, `delayedInterestAmount` 0.00. The loan moves to status `FINISHED`.

If you send the originally scheduled total of EUR 916.20 instead, EUR 12.15 is recorded as an overpayment.

### Early partial repayment

You report EUR 500.00 on 2027-01-30, before installment 1 is due.

Interest is recalculated to the payment date as in the previous example (EUR 4.05) and settled first. The rest of the payment reduces principal immediately. The principal that is paid early is taken from the installments in due-date order, so installment 1 is settled first.

| Step | Claim                                 | Outstanding | Allocated | Remaining payment |
| ---- | ------------------------------------- | ----------- | --------- | ----------------- |
| 1    | Delayed interest                      | 0.00        | 0.00      | 500.00            |
| 2    | Interest (recalculated to 2027-01-30) | 4.05        | 4.05      | 495.95            |
| 3    | Principal, installment 1              | 300.00      | 300.00    | 195.95            |
| 4    | Principal, installment 2              | 300.00      | 195.95    | 0.00              |

Response: `principalAmount` 495.95, `interestAmount` 4.05, `delayedInterestAmount` 0.00.

After the payment the outstanding principal is EUR 404.05, of which 104.05 remains on installment 2 and 300.00 on installment 3. Interest for the remaining periods is recalculated on the reduced balance from the payment date, so the interest due on 2027-03-16 and 2027-04-15 is lower than in the original schedule. Read `getLoan` for the updated amounts rather than relying on the schedule you sent at creation.

### Partial payment on the due date

Installment 1 is due on 2027-02-14. You report EUR 200.00 on that day.

| Step | Claim            | Outstanding | Allocated | Remaining payment |
| ---- | ---------------- | ----------- | --------- | ----------------- |
| 1    | Delayed interest | 0.00        | 0.00      | 200.00            |
| 2    | Interest         | 8.10        | 8.10      | 191.90            |
| 3    | Principal        | 300.00      | 191.90    | 0.00              |

Response: `principalAmount` 191.90, `interestAmount` 8.10, `delayedInterestAmount` 0.00. EUR 108.10 of principal remains outstanding on installment 1. From 2027-02-15 it is overdue and the investor-held part of it accrues delayed interest.

### Payment larger than the installment

Installment 1 is due on 2027-02-14. You report EUR 320.00 on that day.

Installment 1 is settled as in the on-time example (8.10 interest, 300.00 principal). The remaining EUR 11.90 is applied to installment 2 as an early principal payment, in the same way as in the early partial repayment example. The response shows `principalAmount` 311.90 and `interestAmount` 8.10. The outstanding principal of installment 2 drops to EUR 288.10 and its interest is recalculated on that balance from 2027-02-14.

### Overpayment

An overpayment only arises when the payment exceeds everything outstanding on the loan. Installments 1 and 2 are paid. Installment 3 (300.00 principal, 2.70 interest) is due on 2027-04-15 and you report EUR 310.00 on that day. The platform settles 2.70 interest and 300.00 principal, moves the loan to `FINISHED`, and records EUR 7.30 as an overpayment. The response shows `principalAmount` 300.00 and `interestAmount` 2.70 only. Contact your Lendermarket account manager to reconcile the difference.

## Errors

| HTTP | Field           | Message                    | Cause                                                                                     |
| ---- | --------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 422  | `loanUuid`      | `Invalid loan uuid`        | Loan does not exist, does not belong to you, or `currency` differs from the loan currency |
| 422  | `loanUuid`      | `Provided loan not active` | Loan is not in status `ACTIVE`                                                            |
| 422  | `paymentAmount` | validation message         | Amount missing, zero, negative, or with too many decimals                                 |
| 422  | `currency`      | validation message         | Currency missing or not supported                                                         |

## Summary

| Topic            | Rule                                                                                      |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What you send    | The total amount received. The platform allocates it                                      |
| Payment date     | Date of the API call (UTC). No backdating                                                 |
| Order            | Oldest installment first. Within an installment: delayed interest, interest, principal    |
| Interest         | Simple interest, 360-day year, truncated to cents on the cumulative amount                |
| Delayed interest | Loan daily rate on overdue investor-held principal, from the due date to the payment date |
| Early payment    | Interest recalculated to the payment date. Early principal reduces the remaining schedule |
| Partial payment  | Remainder of the installment stays open and, after the due date, accrues delayed interest |
| Overpayment      | Recorded, not allocated, not returned automatically                                       |
